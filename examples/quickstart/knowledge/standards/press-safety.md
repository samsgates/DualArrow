# Press Maintenance Safety Standard

## Energy isolation

Maintenance personnel must disconnect and lock out all electrical, hydraulic, and pneumatic energy sources before opening guarded machine areas.

## Verification

A second qualified person must verify the lockout when maintenance requires removal of the primary drive assembly.
