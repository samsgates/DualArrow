# Press Machine P-104 Operations and Maintenance

## Approved bearing

The approved primary drive bearing for Press Machine P-104 is BR-442. The BR-442 bearing is also approved for the P-104X model. It is not approved for the P-103 model.

## Preventive replacement interval

Replace bearing BR-442 after 5,000 operating hours or when vibration exceeds 7.5 millimetres per second RMS, whichever occurs first.

## Safety preparation

Disconnect and lock out the primary electrical supply before opening the bearing housing. Verify zero stored hydraulic pressure before beginning removal.

## Post-maintenance verification

After bearing replacement, operate the machine without production load for 15 minutes. Stop the machine if bearing temperature exceeds 85 degrees Celsius or vibration exceeds 7.5 millimetres per second RMS.
