# Controlled Experiment Evidence Guide

## Association and causation

An observed association does not by itself establish causation. A causal conclusion requires an appropriate study design, control of relevant confounding variables, and evidence that the proposed cause precedes the measured outcome.

## Reproducibility

A result should be considered more reliable when independent teams reproduce it under comparable experimental conditions.
